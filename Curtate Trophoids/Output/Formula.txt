The term Curtate Trochoid (also frequently called a Curtate Cycloid)
refers to a specific type of curve in geometry, known as a roulette.
It is the path traced by a fixed point that is inside a circle as that circle rolls along a straight line without slipping.

  Think of a bicycle wheel rolling down the street.
If you trace a point on the rim of the tire, you get a regular Cycloid.
If you trace a point inside the rim (like a reflective sticker on the spoke), you get a Curtate Trochoid.
2The formula for the Curtate Trochoid is given by a set of parametric equations which describe the x and y coordinates of the tracing point, p, as a function of the angle the circle has rotated, \theta (theta).

y(\theta) = a - b \cos(\theta)
